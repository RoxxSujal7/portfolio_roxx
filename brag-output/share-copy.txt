Most developer portfolios are broken templates: slow external redirects, 4-second load times, and generic cards.

Today, we're launching Sujal.dev A to Z — an engineering portfolio re-architected like high-throughput production infrastructure.

Here is everything we built, from A to Z 🧵👇

1/ [A is for Art Direction & Kinetic Motion]
60fps Lenis physics, draggable comic stickers, vintage tape peel effects, and custom typography running with zero lag.

2/ [B & C is for Built-in Dev Terminal & Cmd+K Shell]
Hit Cmd+K (or Ctrl+K) anywhere on the page to open a live dev terminal. Type real commands: `sys --info`, `skills`, `projects`, `contact`, and even `celebrate` for confetti.

3/ [D is for Distributed AI & Zero-Bounce Drawers]
Portfolios lose 80% of hiring managers to external links. Our in-page case study slide-over drawers render complete multi-agent pipelines, Qdrant vector memory graphs, and live telemetry (<1.2s latency) in-context.

4/ [E is for Executive Recruiter Moat]
Hiring managers don't have time to read 6 pages. 1-Click ATS Recruiter Mode generates a high-density competency matrix with instant 1-click printable PDF export and tactile clipboard copy.

5/ [Z is for Zero Framework Bloat]
Built with pure vanilla TypeScript + GSAP + Lenis + Astro. 100% WCAG 2.1 AA accessible, verified with automated Playwright test suites.

With great code comes great velocity.
