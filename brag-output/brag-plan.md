# Brag Plan: Sujal · Full Stack & AI Engineer Portfolio

## What is this app?
An interactive, poster-style developer portfolio for Sujal featuring *Into the Spider-Verse* comic art direction, tactile physical interactions, advanced GSAP choreographies, Lenis kinetic smooth scrolling, and an interactive Spidey dark mode with radial transitions.

## The angle
"Spider-Verse Blockbuster Teaser" — treat Sujal's portfolio not as a static resume, but as a high-stakes cinematic film trailer. In a multiverse full of boilerplate templates, one engineer builds autonomous AI agents, high-throughput systems, and silky 60fps kinetic web experiences.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cinematic black letterbox opens with dramatic scale into nocturnal Spider-Verse ink. Colossal cinematic trailer typography fades and slams in: "EVERY MULTIVERSE NEEDS AN ARCHITECT." A low resonance impact bell rings as Sujal's name and title ignite in crimson neon.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Scene 2 (The Comic World & Web Swing):** Spider-Man swings into frame on silk web lines, landing onto the vintage comic paper layout with taped skill badges raining down ("Full-Stack Web", "AI Agents & LLMs", "RAG & Vector DBs").
- **Scene 3 (Superpowers & Flagship Systems):** 3D project cards elevate with real telemetry — Autonomous AI Assistants, RAG Search Engines, Real-Time Platforms — culminating in the live Spidey Dark Mode radial energy wipe that flips the world into glowing red spiderwebs.

## Outro / punchline
Upside-down hanging Spider-Man lowers beside the colossal wordmark `SUJAL.DEV`. The iconic Spider-Verse mantra appears: *"WITH GREAT CODE COMES GREAT VELOCITY."*

## User flow worth showing
1. Landing on the hero section as Spider-Man swings in on silk.
2. Exploring flagship AI systems and full-stack projects with 3D physical elevation.
3. Triggering the interactive Spidey dark mode with dynamic radial energy expanding across the screen.
